3D Room Studio 3D

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Application URL: https://kridsdale.github.io/room-studio-3d/

Room Studio 3D is a static, client-side Three.js 3D room layout planner hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no backend server, runs entirely in your browser, and never collects, sells, or shares your personal data with third parties.

1. Google Account & Google Drive Data Accessed

If you choose to click Sign in with Google inside the Save & Load panel to enable optional cross-device sync, Room Studio 3D requests only the minimum scopes required:

  • openid, email, and profile — used solely to display your name, email address, and profile avatar inside the app UI so you know which Google account is connected.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — used solely to read and write a single hidden configuration file (room-studio-3d-cloud-v2.json) inside your Google Drive's private appDataFolder. Room Studio 3D cannot view, read, modify, or delete any of your regular Google Drive files or folders.

2. What Is Synced vs. Stored Locally Only

  • Synced to your private Google Drive appDataFolder (when signed in): Your room dimensions, placed furniture coordinates, finishes, lighting/camera preferences, named saved room plans, and IKEA catalog metadata for items placed in your room.
  • Stored strictly locally in your browser (NEVER uploaded to Google Drive): Any optional Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Gemini / LLM API key you enter into the AI Layout Planner. API keys are encrypted locally with Web Crypto (AES-GCM-256) in your browser's IndexedDB/localStorage and are explicitly stripped prior to any cloud upload or JSON export.

3. No Backend Storage, Tracking, or Third-Party Sharing

All communication between your browser and Google APIs happens directly from your browser using your OAuth access token. Access tokens are kept in memory only for your active session and are never persisted to any external server. Room Studio 3D uses no analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or third-party data brokers.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Room Studio 3D's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, profiling, or training AI/ML models.

5. Deleting Your Data & Revoking Access

  • Sign out in-app: Open Save → Sign Out to revoke the active browser OAuth session immediately.
  • Revoke Google permissions: Visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Room Studio 3D.
  • Delete cloud data: In Google Drive, open Settings → Manage apps → Room Studio 3D → Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete local data: Clear site storage for kridsdale.github.io in your browser settings.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the developer at Kevin.Ridsdale@gmail.com.
